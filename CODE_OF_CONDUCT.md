# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in VIRTUE WISE a respectful, welcoming, and constructive experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience level, or point of view.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, curious, and considerate.
- Critique ideas and implementations, not people.
- Assume good faith while remaining open to evidence and correction.
- Welcome different perspectives and constructive disagreement.
- Respect privacy and avoid sharing personal or confidential information.
- Focus feedback on improving the project and its impact.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, deliberate intimidation, doxxing, and unwelcome sexual attention are not acceptable. Publishing another person's private information without consent is prohibited.

## Reporting

Contact the project maintainers through the contact method published on the GitHub organization profile. If no private reporting channel is available yet, do not post sensitive details publicly; ask a maintainer for a secure channel. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove or edit harmful content and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation for behavior they consider abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable. Decisions should be proportionate and communicated where practical.

This code of conduct is adapted from common open-source community practices and will be reviewed as the project grows.
